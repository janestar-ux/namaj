🚫 Prohibited Times for Salat

Based on Sahih Muslim 831 — three times when prayer is not permitted

View by Madhab

«نَهَى النَّبِيُّ ﷺ عَنِ الصَّلَاةِ فِي ثَلَاثِ سَاعَاتٍ»

The Prophet ﷺ prohibited prayer at three times: at sunrise, at solar noon (istiwa'), and at sunset. — Sahih Muslim 831, Abu Dawud 3192

Ḥanafī: All voluntary (nafl) and makeup (qaḍā) prayers are strictly prohibited. Fard prayers with a cause (janāzah, sajdah tilāwah) are permitted except at istiwa'. Makrūh Taḥrīmān.
